# Summary
This is a priority queue application for students and professors to queue their questions and topics for office hours. It will have two parts: student and professor. Student part will have a simple input for their question and it will output a number in the queue based on relevance/importance of their question. Professor part will have different features including view of the queue, changing the queue.

# Textstack
- Front end - React
- Back end - Flask
- Database - SQL Light

# Features
## Must-haves
### Student end
- Display the queue
- Input box with type of questions
- Logic for prioritizing queue
- Upload syllabus to get office hour data

### Professor end
- Display the queue
- Change timing and location of office hours
- Push remaining people still waiting once office hours close to next time
- Be able to change queue

## Nice-to-have
- Group similar questions
- TA office hours
- Bump to TA office hours if overflowing in professor office hours or at the beginning depending on question
